# v2.5 Batch 4D

Tours: **Indus Valley Civilization** (5 chapters), **Mauryan Empire** (5), **The Vikings** (5), **The Cold War, 1947-1991** (7), **Decolonisation after 1945** (6), **West African empires** (4). Validated like earlier batches.

## Install
1. Unzip over the repo root. 2. `python apply_batch4d.py` (idempotent). 3. `node test/test-batch4d.js` plus earlier tests. 4. `python server.py`, hard refresh.

## Framing
- Indus: the decline lists the four theories side by side; governance is left an open, undated question.
- Mauryan: two older explanations for the empire's fall are shown as rejected by the source; Ashoka's Buddhism is cross-referenced to the Buddhism tour.
- Cold War: sits beside the Communism tour; it does not repeat the Soviet and Chinese internal history. Unsupported claims (e.g. the Warsaw Pact member list) are omitted rather than guessed.
- Decolonisation: contrasts Ghana (negotiated), Algeria (war) and the Portuguese colonies (wars ending a dictatorship). The Algerian independence dates conflict across sources and the chapter says so.

## Limits
- West African empires is a short outline resting partly on unrated sites; my searches for the Swahili coast returned nothing usable, so it is omitted.
- Vikings omit eastern Europe, Constantinople, society and religion. Decolonisation omits South Africa, Rhodesia, Kenya, the Congo crisis, Asia, the Middle East and the Caribbean.
- Cold War omits arms control, proxy wars outside Korea and Cuba, Vietnam and Afghanistan in detail (Vietnam appears in the Communism tour).
- Sources are encyclopedia excerpts plus a few unrated pages.
