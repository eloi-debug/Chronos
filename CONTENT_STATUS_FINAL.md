## Thin or one-sided
- Several tours are outlines built from short encyclopedia excerpts: West African empires, Mongolia after the empire, British Empire II, Ottoman II, Egypt, Macedonia, China, Hindu traditions, Daoism, Islam I and II. Each says so in its chapters and ends, where relevant, with a scope note.
- Japan's wartime chapter and the Nara/Heian chapter rest on unrated or table-of-contents sources.
- Casualty figures are shown only where sources give them, and then as ranges. The Vietnam War tour gives none and says so.

## Known gaps (not built)
American colonies and settler colonies of the British Empire; Ireland beyond the Famine; South Africa/Rhodesia/Kenya/Congo; Asian and Middle East decolonisation; Balkan Wars, Gallipoli and other victims of WWI-era violence; Islam in Spain, Safavids, Mughals, Fatimids; Hindu bhakti and temples; Tibet; Laos and Cambodia; the Korean War in detail; Africa south of the Sahel beyond the Swahili coast; Korea; Southeast Asian kingdoms (Khmer, Srivijaya); Australia and the Pacific; most of the 20th century outside the Cold War.

## Sources
Roughly 400 source records. Mostly encyclopedias, museums, universities and archives; a number are marked secondary-unrated. No peer-reviewed article was read in full. Statements resting on unrated pages say so in the chapter.

## Not verified
Coordinates are approximate and many regions are broad circles, not borders. Tours have not been fact-checked by a historian.
