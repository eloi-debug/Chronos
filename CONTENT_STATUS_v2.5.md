## Depth: where coverage is thin or one-sided
- Outline-level only: Egypt, Macedonia, China, Mongol, Ottoman, Islam, Hindu traditions, Daoism, Persia (Parthians). Each says so in its chapters.
- Missing periods: Islam after 750; Ottoman 17th–19th centuries and WWI; China Five Dynasties/northern dynasties/Liao/Jin and the Republic; Mongolia after the 14th century; Christianity outside Europe and the Levant; Hindu bhakti, temples and spread abroad; Daoism after the Tang; Buddhism in India after Ashoka.
- British Empire: American colonies, Ireland, Opium Wars, settler colonies and Indigenous peoples, famines, mandates and the Commonwealth are unbuilt.
- Nazi tour: camps are not mapped; resistance and occupied peoples' experience are not covered.
- Religious tours describe history and texts, not doctrine; faith claims are labelled as such.
