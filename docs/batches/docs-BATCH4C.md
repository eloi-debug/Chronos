# v2.5 Batch 4C: Americas and Japan

Tours: **The Maya** (5 chapters), **The Aztec Empire** (5), **The Inca Empire** (5), **Japan: from Yamato to the modern state** (9). Validated like earlier batches.

## Install
1. Unzip over the repo root. 2. `python apply_batch4c.py` (idempotent). 3. `node test/test-batch4c.js` plus earlier tests. 4. `python server.py`, hard refresh.

## Framing
- Maya: the 9th-century collapse is treated as the collapse of lowland city-states, not the disappearance of a people; the final chapter says the Maya continued. Collapse causes are listed as proposals, not decided.
- Aztec: the founding legend is labelled traditional; the conquest chapters name indigenous allies and smallpox alongside Spanish weapons. Because the Triple Alliance was an alliance, the tour says the 'empire' is not a single centralised state.
- Inca: the tour states that the Inca had no writing, uses Spanish-era accounts with a warning, and does not claim the Inca ended in 1533.
- Japan: early (Nara/Heian) and wartime chapters are thin and rely on a table of contents or one unrated blog; they say so. It does not describe wartime atrocities, occupation regimes in Asia, or Japanese civilian suffering, which need better sources and care.

## Limits
- Sources are encyclopedia excerpts (a few unrated). Not tested in a real browser.
- Coordinates are approximate; the Inca 'extent' line is a schematic axis, not a border.
- Not covered: Aztec tribute and religion; Maya writing and calendar; Inca religion and administration beyond roads; Japan's religion, culture and economy.
