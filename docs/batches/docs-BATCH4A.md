# v2.5 Batch 4A: Modern history

Tours: **The Russian Empire, 1721–1917** (7 chapters), **Communism: origins, spread and downfall** (9 chapters). Validated like earlier batches.

## Install
1. Unzip over the repo root. 2. `python apply_batch4a.py` (idempotent). 3. `node test/test-batch4a.js` plus the earlier tests. 4. `python server.py`, hard refresh.

## Framing
- The communism tour separates the idea (Marx and Engels), the party-states (USSR, Eastern Bloc, China, Cuba, Vietnam) and the downfall. It states repression, famines and mass deaths as the sources record them, with ranges side by side where estimates differ (e.g. four different Great Leap Forward famine estimates are listed as alternatives). It makes no claim on whether the Holodomor was genocide.
- The Russian Empire tour is thin where my sources are thin: the 19th century chapter (1812, Decembrists, emancipation, Alexander II) says so openly and does not fill the gap from memory.

## Limits
- Sources are encyclopedia excerpts (a few unrated). Not tested in a real browser.
- Communism: no Laos, Cambodia, Africa, Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia 1968, Hungary 1956, North Korea's later history, Tiananmen 1989, or post-1991 communist states beyond brief notes for China and Cuba.
- Russian Empire: no Siberia/Central Asia conquest, Alexander I–III, the Crimean War, or Russo-Turkish wars.
