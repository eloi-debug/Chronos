# v2.5 Batch 4B: part-2 tours

Tours: **The British Empire II: famine, opium and the costs of rule** (4 chapters), **The Ottoman Empire II: reform, revolution and the Armenian Genocide** (5), **Islam II: Karbala, Baghdad and the spread by trade** (6). Validated like earlier batches.

## Install
1. Unzip over the repo root. 2. `python apply_batch4b.py` (idempotent). 3. `node test/test-batch4b.js` plus earlier tests. 4. `python server.py`, hard refresh.

## Framing
- Death tolls are shown as the ranges the sources give (Irish Famine ~1 million; Bengal 0.8-3.8 million; Armenian Genocide 600,000-1.5 million) with the disagreements side by side. Where responsibility is debated (Ireland, Bengal) the tour presents the positions and does not adjudicate.
- The Armenian Genocide chapters use the term as Britannica, the US Holocaust Memorial Museum and Wikipedia do. I did not research current official positions.
- Each tour ends with an undated, unmapped scope note listing what is not covered.

## Limits
- British Empire II is short (3 episodes + scope note). I searched for the American colonies, settler colonies, Caribbean, mandates and Kenya but got too little to build chapters on.
- Ottoman II omits the Balkan Wars, Arab provinces, Gallipoli and other victim groups.
- Islam II omits Fatimids, Seljuks, Mughals, Safavids, Spain, theology and law.
- Sources are encyclopedia excerpts and a few unrated pages. Not tested in a real browser.
