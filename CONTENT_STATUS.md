## Design decisions
- Tours start paused ("Guided"), so users press Play for auto-play. Auto-play, paused and manual exploration have separate badges.
- Border data is shown only as the nearest earlier snapshot with the gap in years stated. No interpolation or cross-fading.
- Myth features may never be "exact"; features with no defensible place are listed as "unmapped" in the card and not drawn. Myth chapters can be undated and unmapped (Asgard is both).
- Straight lines between places are labelled "schematic" and never represent routes.
- Feature kinds: direct, alliance, tributary, occupation, influence, diffusion, conflict, site, myth. Precision: exact, approximate, regional, illustrative, unmapped. Evidence: attested, reconstructed, traditional, disputed, mythological, speculative.
